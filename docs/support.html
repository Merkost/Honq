FAQ

Is Honq free?
Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, ever.

Which states are supported?
In-app question banks for NSW and NT. Direct links to official resources for VIC, QLD, SA, WA, TAS, and ACT.

Is Honq affiliated with any government agency?
No. Honq is an independent study app. Always check official sources for the latest information.
