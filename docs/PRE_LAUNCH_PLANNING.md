# Honq Pre-Launch Planning Guide

## Executive Summary

**Honq** is a Kotlin Multiplatform driving knowledge test preparation app for Australian drivers. This document outlines recommended features, improvements, marketing strategy, and pricing before launching to the App Store and Google Play.

---

## Part 1: Additional Features Before Launch

### 1.1 High Priority (Must-Have for MVP)

#### 1. Review Incorrect Answers
- After completing a mock test, allow users to review only the questions they got wrong
- Show the correct answer with detailed explanation
- Option to add incorrect questions to favorites for later practice
- **Why**: This is the core learning loop - users need to learn from mistakes

#### 2. Category/Topic Filtering
- Let users practice questions by specific category:
  - Road Rules
  - Traffic Signs
  - Road Safety
  - Alcohol & Fatigue
  - Intersections & Roundabouts
  - Speed Limits
  - Parking
- Show progress per category
- **Why**: Targeted practice helps users focus on weak areas

#### 3. Question Difficulty Indicator
- Display difficulty level (Easy/Medium/Hard) on questions
- Filter practice by difficulty
- Start with easier questions, progress to harder ones
- **Why**: Builds confidence and creates a learning curve

#### 4. Search Functionality
- Search questions by keyword
- Search within favorites
- **Why**: Quick access to specific topics users want to review

#### 5. Settings Screen
- Notification preferences (study reminders)
- Sound effects toggle
- Haptic feedback toggle
- Theme selection (Dark/Light/System)
- Clear progress option
- About/Legal information
- **Why**: Basic user control expected in all apps

### 1.2 Medium Priority (Launch Week Updates)

#### 6. Study Reminders (Push Notifications)
- Daily reminder to practice
- Customizable reminder time
- Smart reminders based on usage patterns
- Streak notifications ("Don't break your 5-day streak!")
- **Why**: Increases retention and daily active users

#### 7. Streak System
- Track consecutive days of practice
- Visual streak counter on home screen
- Streak milestones with celebrations
- Streak freeze (1 per week for premium users)
- **Why**: Gamification drives engagement

#### 8. Statistics Dashboard
- Total questions practiced
- Overall accuracy percentage
- Accuracy by category (radar chart)
- Mock test history with scores
- Time spent studying
- Improvement over time graph
- **Why**: Users want to see their progress visualized

#### 9. Question Explanation Images
- Add diagrams for traffic situations
- Sign images with annotations
- Visual explanations for complex rules
- **Why**: Visual learning aids comprehension

#### 10. Accessibility Features
- VoiceOver/TalkBack support
- Dynamic text sizing
- High contrast mode
- Reduced motion option
- **Why**: Legal requirement and larger audience reach

### 1.3 Differentiators (Post-Launch)

#### 11. Spaced Repetition Algorithm
- Intelligently schedule question reviews based on memory science
- Questions you struggle with appear more frequently
- Optimizes study time for maximum retention
- **Why**: This is a major differentiator from competitors

#### 12. AI-Powered Explanations
- Tap for deeper explanations of any question
- Ask follow-up questions about road rules
- Contextual learning ("Why is this the rule?")
- **Why**: Modern AI feature that adds significant value

#### 13. Exam Anxiety Mode
- Simulated test environment with realistic timer
- Calming breathing exercises before tests
- Tips for test day
- **Why**: Addresses a real user pain point

#### 14. Multi-State Support
- Expand to VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, NT, ACT
- Each state has its own question bank
- State-specific rules highlighted
- **Why**: 10x your addressable market

#### 15. Social Features
- Share progress on social media
- Challenge friends to mock tests
- Leaderboards (weekly/monthly/all-time)
- **Why**: Viral growth and engagement

## Part 4: Pricing Strategy

### 4.1 Recommended Model: Freemium with Premium

#### Free Tier
- Unlimited practice mode
- 1 free mock test per day
- Basic progress tracking
- Question explanations
- Favorites (up to 20)

#### Premium (One-Time Purchase: $9.99 AUD)
- Unlimited mock tests
- Detailed statistics dashboard
- Spaced repetition learning
- Unlimited favorites
- Category filtering
- Study reminders
- Ad-free experience
- Priority support

### 4.2 Alternative Pricing Models

#### Option A: Subscription
- $3.99/month or $19.99/year
- **Pros**: Recurring revenue, higher LTV
- **Cons**: Users may resist for test prep (one-time need)

#### Option B: Completely Free (Ad-Supported)
- Interstitial ads between questions
- Banner ads on home screen
- **Pros**: Maximum downloads
- **Cons**: Poor UX, low revenue per user (~$0.50-2.00)

#### Option C: Paid App ($4.99)
- No free tier, all features included
- **Pros**: Simple, filters serious users
- **Cons**: Much lower download volume

### 4.3 Recommended: Freemium (One-Time)
**Reasoning:**
1. Users only need the app for 2-4 weeks (test prep)
2. Subscription feels unfair for short-term need
3. One-time purchase converts well for study apps
4. $9.99 is an impulse purchase for anxious test-takers
5. Free tier drives reviews and word-of-mouth

### 4.4 Competitor Pricing (Australia)
| App | Price | Model |
|-----|-------|-------|
| DriveTest NSW | Free | Ad-supported |
| DKT NSW Test | $6.99 | One-time |
| Driving Theory Test AU | $4.99 | One-time |
| L's Test NSW | Free | Freemium + ads |

**Strategy**: Position at premium price ($9.99) with superior quality and features.
